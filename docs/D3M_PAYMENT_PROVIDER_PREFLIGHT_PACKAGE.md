# D3M Payment Provider Preflight Package

## Status

Docs-only preflight outreach package. **Outreach package prepared in follow-on phase** — see `docs/D3M_PROVIDER_PREFLIGHT_OUTREACH_PACKAGE.md`. **No email was sent in this phase.** No provider application or dashboard setup was performed.

Production billing remains **NO-GO**.

## Purpose

Provide a reusable, safe preflight outreach package for new payment providers after FastSpring final decline — category acceptance must be confirmed in writing before any long onboarding process.

## Product Summary

Dreemi is an AI-assisted children's storytelling SaaS for adult account owners such as parents, guardians, families, and educators. It creates child-friendly stories and may include tightly controlled story illustrations. It is not a marketplace, not adult content, not gambling, not financial services, not physical goods, not creator payouts, and not a public social network.

## Risk-Sensitive Facts To Disclose

- AI-assisted story generation.
- Children-focused use case.
- Parent/guardian-managed accounts.
- Optional / controlled story illustrations.
- No child account login required.
- No public story sharing in paid launch.
- No marketplace or creator payouts.
- No standalone open-ended image generator in recommended paid launch packaging.

## Mandatory Questions

1. Can you support onboarding an AI-assisted children's storytelling SaaS?
2. Can you support parent/guardian-managed child profile use cases?
3. Does controlled story illustration / image generation create a prohibited category issue?
4. Can you support a Saudi Arabia-based individual founder / natural-person seller, or is a registered company required?
5. Can you support subscription billing / MoR handling for this product type?
6. What would be required before activation?

## Preferred Short Email

**Draft only — do not send automatically.**

Subject: Preflight question — AI-assisted children's storytelling SaaS

```text
Hi,

Before starting a full onboarding process, I would like to confirm whether your platform can support this product category.

Dreemi is an AI-assisted children's storytelling SaaS for adult account owners such as parents, guardians, families, and educators. It creates child-friendly stories and may include tightly controlled story illustrations. It is not a marketplace, not adult content, not gambling, not financial services, not physical goods, and does not involve creator payouts.

The paid launch can be packaged as story-first, with no public sharing and no standalone open-ended image generator.

Could you please confirm:
1. Whether this product category can be considered for onboarding.
2. Whether AI-assisted children's storytelling is acceptable under your risk policies.
3. Whether controlled story illustrations / image generation create a restricted category issue.
4. Whether a Saudi Arabia-based individual founder can onboard, or whether a registered company is required.
5. Whether you support subscription SaaS / MoR billing for this type of product.

I am happy to provide the website, demo, safety controls, privacy policy, and product details if the category is potentially acceptable.

Best regards,
Hayssam
```

## Expanded Compliance Summary

- Adult account ownership; child profiles managed by parent/guardian.
- Privacy policy, terms, refund policy, and child-safety pages documented on website.
- AI-assisted content with safety controls; not open-ended public generation in paid launch v1 packaging.
- Subscription SaaS for story generation limits/features — not physical fulfillment.
- Saudi Arabia-based individual founder unless provider requires registered company.

## Do Not Include

- Tax IDs, passport/national ID numbers, bank details, payout details
- Private provider account IDs, order IDs, checkout URLs, screenshots
- Unverified claims of legal approval or production readiness
- Full technical webhook/API implementation details before category acceptance

## Evaluation Criteria

| Response | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Clear category acceptance in writing | Proceed to single-provider setup planning phase |
| Conditional acceptance (e.g., story-only, no illustrations) | Record conditions; evaluate against `D3M_PAID_LAUNCH_RISK_REDUCTION_DECISION.md` |
| Requires registered company | Record requirement; evaluate entity timing separately |
| Category declined | Disqualify; do not start dashboard setup |
| No response | Follow up once; then evaluate next candidate |
