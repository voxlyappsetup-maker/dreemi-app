## Dreemi authorized use cases

Higgsfield AI is approved shared creative production infrastructure where it materially reduces cost/time, raises quality, removes a blocker, improves testability, or creates reusable media.

Authorized examples include:

- storyboards and visual concepts;
- motion/cinematic prototypes;
- presentation/explainer assets;
- social/marketing visuals where otherwise permitted;
- thumbnails/covers;
- localization variants;
- reusable non-sensitive creative media.

This authorization does **not** weaken any project-specific child/content/privacy/compliance safeguard. Those stricter gates remain controlling.

## Authorized without a new owner gate

- evaluation;
- prompt development;
- storyboard/workflow design;
- concept/prototype generation;
- creative testing;
- technical workflow design;
- use of official ChatGPT/Claude/Higgsfield integrations;
- production using credits/subscription already approved for the portfolio.

## Separate owner approval still required

- new paid subscription;
- subscription-tier change;
- additional credit-pack purchase;
- annual commitment;
- unbounded API spend;
- public publication when no existing publish authorization covers it;
- paid campaign launch;
- ad spend.

## Quality and safety gate

Higgsfield output is never automatically publish-ready. Applicable QA must cover accuracy, brand consistency, Arabic/RTL, rendered text, product fidelity, screenshots/logos, human anatomy, lip sync/audio, copyright/licensing, platform policy, AI disclosure, child/content/privacy safeguards, and final aspect ratio/resolution.
